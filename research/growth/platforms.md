# Platform mechanics and the funnel to YouTube (appendix A)

Researched 28 Sep 2026 for Wild Card Commander. The synthesis and plan are in `REPORT.md`; this appendix keeps the evidence.

Tags:
- **[official]:** the platform's own help, blog, newsroom or executives.
- **[data]:** a study with a stated sample.
- **[practitioner]:** creator, agency or tool-vendor advice.
- **[unverified]:** repeated claims with no primary source found.

## Headline findings

- **YouTube raises the entry bar on Feb 1, 2027.** New Partner Program applicants will need 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views.
- **Shorts-feed views never count toward watch hours.**
- **Many of the useful Shorts tools are partner-only:** custom Shorts thumbnails, Shorts series and Hype.
- **Our generic TTS narrator needs no disclosure on YouTube or TikTok.** On Meta (Instagram and Facebook) it's a grey area ("realistic-sounding audio").

## 1. YouTube Shorts

### Ranking and view counting
- **Ranking:** "viewed vs swiped away" is YouTube's own Shorts metric in Studio. [official]
- **View counting:** since 31 Mar 2025 a view counts on start or replay. The old count is now "engaged views", which is what the Partner Program and revenue use. [official]
- **Retention:** about 65 % for Shorts under 30 s and about 50 % for 30–60 s are commonly repeated targets. [practitioner]

### Do Shorts hurt long-form?
- YouTube runs separate recommendation systems for Shorts and long-form that "understand cross-format viewing", and has "not seen evidence of Shorts negatively impacting long-form performance". [official, blog, 6 May 2025]
- Its Help pages say trying Shorts "will not inherently confuse the algorithm". [official]

### The related video link
- It's the one tappable bridge from a Short to a long video. It needs advanced features (phone or ID verification). [official]
- **Since the June 2026 redesign** it sits in a swipeable strip under the title, together with the audio track. [reporting]
- **YouTube's conversion guide** (14 Jul 2026) [official] says:
  - link each Short to a video that matches its intent;
  - put a spoken and on-screen CTA in the final 5 s;
  - pay off the Short's promise in the long video's first 5–10 s, with "no slow channel graphics, logos".
- **Links in Shorts descriptions and comments aren't clickable** (since 31 Aug 2023), pinned comments included. [official]

### Length
- Square or vertical videos of 3 min or less count as Shorts (since 15 Oct 2024). [official]
- From 24 Sep 2026, Shorts of 1–3 min with a Content ID claim are no longer auto-blocked. [official]
- Retention beats length. 20–45 s is typical, with 60–180 s only if the video keeps delivering. [practitioner]

### Shorts from our own long videos
- "Remix → Edit into a Short" takes up to 60 s of our own long video, and the Short is automatically linked back to it. [official]

### Partner-only features
- **Custom Shorts thumbnails** (24 Jul 2026): desktop only and not A/B tested. They show on the Shorts tab, Home, Search and TV, but not in the Shorts feed. [official/reporting]
- **Shorts series** (23 Sep 2026): seasons and episodes, rolling out to partners. [official]
- **Hype:** partners with 500–500k subscribers, long-form only, within the first 7 days. [official]

### Other YouTube tools
- **Test & Compare:** 3 variants, and the winner is chosen by *watch-time share*. Desktop only; not for Shorts. [official]
- **Ask Studio** is replacing the Inspiration tab (from Aug 2026). [official/reporting]
- **Posts** need no subscriber minimum (only advanced features). They reach Home and Subscriptions, and are now expanding into the Shorts feed. [official/reporting]
- **Viewers can turn Shorts off** (since 21 Apr 2026, by setting Shorts time to 0 min). [reporting]

### Long-form on a new channel
- YouTube judges each video on its own; new channels need time to build credibility, and a bigger library helps. [official]
- **Cadence:** Metricool 2026 (799,718 videos) found long-form views up 76 % year on year, Shorts making up 61 % of views, and recommends 2–4 videos a week. [data]
- **Official line on cadence:** no per-video boost from posting more; keep a sustainable rhythm. [official-adjacent]
- **Subscribers:** long-form earned 22.7 subscribers per 10k views against 16.9 for Shorts (Galloway, 2023). [data]

## 2. TikTok

- **The For You feed ranks on** interactions, video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and, lightly, device settings. Finishing a longer video counts strongly. Follower count isn't a direct factor. [official, 2020]
- **US algorithm:** since 22 Jan 2026 it's controlled by the new US joint venture and being retrained on US data, so older benchmarks may not hold. [official/reporting]
- **Not eligible for the For You feed** [official, Community Guidelines, 2025]:
  - reused or unoriginal content, including other platforms' watermarks or logos;
  - low-quality or minimally edited content;
  - engagement bait;
  - LIVE content whose main purpose is sending people off TikTok.
- **Mentioning YouTube:** there's no rule and no test showing that ordinary videos which mention YouTube are suppressed. [unverified myth] The real risks are bait-style CTAs and the drop-off a hard-sell ending causes. [practitioner]
- **AI labels:**
  - "Disclosure isn't needed when… using generic text-to-speech (TTS) narration" or "artistic styles, like anime". [official]
  - TikTok auto-labels AI content from C2PA credentials.
  - Since Nov 2025, users have a "see less AI" slider. [official]
- **Creator Rewards:**
  - Needs 10,000 followers, 100k views in 30 days, a personal account, and videos over 1 minute. [official]
  - Payouts weigh originality, play time, search value and engagement. [practitioner]
- **Bio link:** 1,000 followers on a personal account, or immediately on a Business account, which loses the full music library. Sources conflict; check in the app. [mixed]
- **Length:** Buffer (1.1M posts) found 3–10 min videos got the most views on average. [data]
- **Search:** it reads captions, speech (auto-transcript) and on-screen text (OCR). Say, show and write the keyword early. Hashtags matter little now. [practitioner]
- **Cadence:** 3–5 posts a week. [data]

## 3. Instagram Reels (and Facebook)

- **Ranking:** watch time, likes and sends are the top three signals, per Adam Mosseri, Instagram's head. Watch *sends per reach*. [official, Jan 2025]
- **Originality:**
  - Originals replace reposts in recommendations. Accounts that repost others' content 10+ times in 30 days lose recommendation eligibility. [official, 2024]
  - From Apr 2026 this covers photos and carousels too. [official]
  - Voiceover and commentary count as original; watermarks and recycled clips from other apps are not recommended. [official]
- **Length:** Reels up to 3 min are recommended like shorter ones. [official, Jan 2025]
- **Trial Reels** (Dec 2024): shown to non-followers first, then auto-shared to followers if they do well. [official]
- **Hashtags:** capped at 5 per post (Dec 2025), and they don't really add reach. [official]
- **Links:**
  - 5 bio links, plus Story link stickers.
  - Links in Reel captions aren't clickable, apart from a Meta Verified test (Mar 2026). [reporting]
- **Facebook:** Business or Creator accounts can cross-post Reels to Facebook Reels. Meta says views of *original* Facebook Reels roughly doubled in the second half of 2025. [official] Meta cracked down on unoriginal and duplicate content in July 2025. [official]
- **AI labels:** Meta requires its AI-disclosure label for photorealistic video or *realistic-sounding audio*, so our narrator is a grey area. Meta hasn't said the label reduces reach. [official/unverified]
- **Instagram as a traffic source for YouTube:** there's no study. The path is profile, then bio link or Story sticker, typically 3–5 % profile visits and 10–20 % link taps. Expect a trickle. [practitioner]

## 4. Posting the same vertical video to all three

- All three demote other platforms' watermarks and logos. Our own logo or mascot is fine. [official]
- Posting our own original video to all three isn't "reposting"; the originality rules target aggregators. [official, by reading the policies]
- Export clean masters from Remotion, upload natively to each app, and use each app's own caption and text tools. Vary the first frame's hook text and the final CTA per platform, and keep text inside each app's safe zone. [practitioner]

## 5. Faceless channels, AI voice and Partner Program risk

### YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy
It was renamed from "repetitious content" on 15 Jul 2025. [official, Help 1311392]
- **Allowed:** the same intro and outro when the bulk of each video differs; a series where each video has a distinct storyline or concept.
- **Not allowed:**
  - repetitive content with low educational value;
  - "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates" without the creator's own insight;
  - near-identical narrated slideshows.
- YouTube called it a clarification; AI itself isn't banned. [official]
- "Managing AI slop" is a 2026 priority, handled by the existing spam systems. [official, Jan 2026]

### YouTube's AI disclosure
- It's required only for *realistic* altered or synthetic content.
- It's **not** required for animation, AI help with the script or outline, or cloning your own voice.
- Disclosing "won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money". [official]

### Partner Program thresholds
- **Now** [official]:
  - Lower tier: 500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days.
  - Full tier: 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views.
  - Views in the Shorts feed don't count toward watch hours.
- **From 1 Feb 2027, for new applicants:** 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days, or 20M Shorts views in 90 days. Current partners are unaffected. [official, blog, 10 Aug 2026]

### What keeps our channel safe (synthesis)
- an original script and thesis per episode;
- original animation and a consistent mascot;
- a distinct concept per video, never a template where only the card names change;
- visible human choices and opinions;
- a generic (non-cloned) TTS voice;
- Meta's AI label switched on;
- no third-party footage;
- WotC card art under the permission letter, kept on file.

## 6. Moving viewers from the short platforms to YouTube

**Works (strongest evidence first):**
1. The related video link, plus a spoken and on-screen pointer in the last 5 s, plus a long video that pays off the Short at once. [official]
2. Value-first Shorts that are complete in themselves, with "the full version" offered. These beat cliffhanger "part 2 on YouTube" endings, which risk bait rules and completion drops. [official + practitioner]
3. A recognizable series and character across apps. [data + practitioner]
4. Owned links: the YouTube channel link, TikTok and Instagram bio links, and Instagram Story stickers. Expect low single-digit percentages at each step. [practitioner]
5. Outside traffic that bounces quickly can hurt the long video; it only helps if those viewers keep watching. [practitioner]

**Doesn't work:**
- links in Shorts descriptions or comments;
- watermarked cross-posts;
- hashtag stuffing;
- hard-sell endings;
- deleting and re-uploading Shorts.

## The agent's top 15 recommendations

1. Long-form watch hours are the Partner Program path; Shorts are the funnel, not the revenue.
2. Verify the channel now, to unlock the related link, Test & Compare, Posts and custom long-form thumbnails.
3. Every Short gets a related link to its matching episode, plus a spoken and on-screen pointer in the last 5 s.
4. Every episode pays off its Short's promise in the first 5–10 s.
5. Shorts are value-first and 30–60 s. On TikTok, also cut 61–90 s versions for Creator Rewards later.
6. Render per-platform masters: no watermarks, native captions, per-platform hook text and CTA, safe zones.
7. Put Wil in the first frame of every Short. Give recurring series names, and use YouTube's Shorts series once in the Partner Program.
8. Guard against the inauthentic-content policy: a documented thesis per episode, varied structure, and kept script drafts.
9. AI voice: no disclosure on YouTube or TikTok for generic TTS; turn the label on for Instagram and Facebook and track reach.
10. Run Test & Compare with 3 thumbnails on every long-form upload.
11. Upload long-form weekly and Shorts 3–5 times a week, each pointing at a recent episode.
12. TikTok: stay on a personal account (Creator Rewards needs it) and grow to 1,000 followers for the bio link.
13. Instagram:
    - make Reels people will send to friends;
    - use Trial Reels once eligible;
    - use at most 3–5 hashtags;
    - put 5 bio links, starting with the latest episode;
    - add Story stickers on upload days;
    - cross-post to Facebook Reels.
14. Say and show the commander or card name in the first 3 s, and put it first in the caption.
15. Measure the funnel monthly and drop whatever doesn't move long-form watch hours.
