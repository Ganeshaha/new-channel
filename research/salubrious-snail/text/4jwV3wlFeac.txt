# Is Enlightened Tutor a draw spell?
channel: Salubrious Snail | https://youtu.be/4jwV3wlFeac
date: 2025-05-14 | views: 22,581 | likes: 929 | comments: 42 | length: 0:47 | words: 182 | wpm: 234.1

[0:00] Would you consider enlightened tutor a draw spell? I mean, it's literally card negative, so that would be stupid, right? It actually depends. For one, if you almost always use it to find something that draws a lot of cards, like Skull Clamp or Wristic Study, it essentially takes on the function of a draw spell. Though, it's a worse top deck since it puts the card on top. Secondly, whether or not a tutor should be thought of as card draw really depends on what task draw spells are accomplishing in your deck. If you're running draw spells like Esper Sentinel and whirlwind of thought mainly to find a key card like Jessica Ascendency or Isaconceptor, a tutor like Enlightened Tutor is accomplishing the same task and differentiating them is pretty pointless. It's a difference without a distinction. Does this mean that tutors are always interchangeable with draw? Absolutely not.
