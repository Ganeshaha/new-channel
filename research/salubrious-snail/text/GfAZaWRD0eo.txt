[0:00] Would you consider Corser of Cruix a draw spell? Actually, this one's an unambiguous yes from me. It digs deeper into your library by getting lands out of the way, and it creates card advantage by letting you play lands from library instead of from your hand. That's a draw engine. Now, you might think, is it really card advantage to play lands off the top and then let lands pile up in your hand instead? And yes, yes, it is. Anytime you play a land off the top, imagine instead that it went to your hand and you played a land from your hand instead. Those lands piling up in hand would be there if you drew the cards instead. They just might not be the exact same lands.
