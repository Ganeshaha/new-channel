# Shorts that promote a long video: research and what we do

Researched 27 Sep 2026 for the ep002 promo Short.

**Source tags:**
- **[P]** YouTube or Google primary.
- **[D]** data study.
- **[PR]** practitioner advice.
- **⚠** unverified, weakly sourced, or older than 2024.

**Our own MTG evidence** (`research/*/FINDINGS.md`):
- Attack on Cardboard's Shorts drew 67% of its views.
- Its "RULES QUESTION:" and "Wizards broke X" Shorts had a 77k median, against 8k for its other Shorts.
- The winning format: one question on screen, one card, the answer inside a minute.

## Conclusions

- **Length: 40–55 s, under 60.**
  - A two-card loop needs about 25–30 s. The catch takes 8–10 s and the call to action (CTA) 4–6 s.
  - Past 60 s adds swipe risk with no benefit to the link.
  - [P] Shorts can run to 3 min (Oct 2024). Ranking uses "viewed vs swiped away", average % viewed, likes and surveys, and Shorts "can't hurt your Video performance" (Help 11914225).
  - [D⚠] Galloway 2023 (5,400 Shorts): 50–60 s did best; a healthy viewed-vs-swiped is 70–90%, and under 60% underperforms.
- **The Related video link is the only clickable route to the long video.**
  - [P] Links in Shorts descriptions and comments stopped being clickable on 31 Aug 2023.
  - [P] You set it in Studio → Content → the Short → Related video. It needs advanced features, and the target must be public or unlisted (Help 14075157).
  - [P] Since the June 2026 player redesign it sits in a swipeable carousel under the title.
  - One link per Short.
- **YouTube's own playbook** [P], Blog, 14 Jul 2026:
  - Use explicit spoken or visual cues in the final 5 seconds.
  - "Physically point downward toward the location of the Related Video link" and "flash a brief overlay of the long-form video's thumbnail".
  - The long video's first 5–10 s should address what the Short promised. Ours does: the cold open is the same hook.
- **Expect low conversion.**
  - [P] Help 16559651 says cross-format watching is "not common behavior from viewers".
  - [PR] Roberto Blake: many Shorts point at one long video over 1–3 weeks.
  - No reliable public click-through numbers exist.
  - So the Short has to be good on its own, and more than one Short should point at each long video.
- **Structure: pay off the headline promise, then open a second question** that the link answers.
  - Our version: the combo is fully shown, and "the catch + how to stop it" stays open.
  - [P] YouTube: Shorts "should also be enjoyable on their own", "get to the point quickly", and end with a "watch the full video" call.
  - [P] Jenny Hoyos: "you have one second to hook someone".
- **The vertical safe area** has no official figures ([PR] consensus):
  - Keep clear of the top ~250 px and the right rail (x ≥ 880, roughly y 850–1650).
  - Keep clear of the bottom ~420 px (handle, title, the carousel with the link, progress bar).
  - That leaves a safe box of about **x 60–880, y 250–1500**.
  - Nothing important goes bottom-right.
  - On a white page, a faint grey scrim at the bottom and right keeps YouTube's white interface text legible.
- **Captions:**
  - [P] "Shorts are often watched without sound, so captions are essential" (Blog, Apr 2025).
  - [PR] Auto-captions are off by default in the Shorts player.
  - Recommendation: burned-in keyword captions of 1–3 words at 60–80 px or larger, plus the SRT. **Adopted for all Shorts** (owner, 27 Sep 2026). The Short's stickers then must not repeat the captions.
- **Audio:**
  - An AI narrator is fine; templated sameness is the risk.
  - Music or trending sound is optional for an explainer. We use no music, consistent with the long-form.
- **Metadata:**
  - Title 40 characters or fewer, different from the long-form title.
  - 2–3 hashtags. `#shorts` is optional, since length and aspect ratio classify a Short.
  - Custom Shorts thumbnails (Jul 2026) are YPP-only, desktop-only, not A/B-tested, and not shown in the feed. Until YPP, pick a frame.
  - Shorts series (Sep 2026) are rolling out to YPP creators.
- **Timing:**
  - Publish the long video first, then the Short the same day or within 24–48 h, so the link works at once.
  - Then post 2–3 more Shorts from the episode over 1–2 weeks, all linking to it.
- **Policy:**
  - [P] Cutting your own original long video into Shorts isn't "reused content".
  - The "inauthentic content" risk is mass-produced, near-identical Shorts. So vary the formats, and never swap card names into one fixed template.
- **Measure at 7 and 28 days:**
  - viewed vs swiped (70% or more is good);
  - engaged views;
  - average % viewed;
  - the long video's traffic from Shorts.

## The promo-Short template we use (ep002)

| Time | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | The claim is on screen at frame 0 ("THIS PRECON WINS WITH ONE CARD" plus the product), with the first word at 0.27 s: the long video's own hook, then "Wizards messed up." | Hook in the first second; mirrors the long video's opening |
| 8–14 s | The key card, big, with its one relevant ability as keyword text | Setup |
| 14–32 s | The mechanism on a vertical stage (OPPONENT top, YOU bottom), a new beat every 2–3 s | Delivers the promise |
| 32–37 s | The payoff: "YOUR BOARD NOW", ×∞ | The satisfying end state |
| 37–40 s | "But there's a catch": a face-down "?" card | Opens the second question |
| 40–43 s | CTA: the "?" becomes the long video's thumbnail, "THE CATCH + HOW TO STOP IT", "FULL VIDEO ↓", Wil points down at the link. No new voice line | YouTube's playbook: point at the link, flash the thumbnail |

The narration is cut from the long video (`video/scripts/short_cut.py`), so there's no TTS cost and the voice matches. Build steps are in WORKFLOW.md under "Shorts".
